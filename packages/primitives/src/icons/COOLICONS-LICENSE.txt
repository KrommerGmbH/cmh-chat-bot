Changes made (CC BY 4.0 requires a change notice)

- The path values (d) are used unchanged. ChatIcon draws them with a default stroke width of 1.5 (the original is 2).
- The "duotone" and "duocolor" variants do not exist in the free coolicons set. They are our own substitute: the same line drawn again as a faint layer behind it. They are not the drawings of the paid "coolicons PRO".
